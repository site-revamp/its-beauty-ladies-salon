It's Beauty — Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
It's Beauty is a ladies-only beauty salon and hair studio operating across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, UAE. The business offers a wide range of professional beauty services, including hair styling, colouring, nail care, brows, lashes and makeup.

The redesigned homepage targets women looking for professional beauty treatments, regular salon maintenance and special-occasion services.

The new design follows The Beauty Atelier creative direction, combining an elegant editorial aesthetic with a clearer, more conversion-focused browsing and booking experience.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Booking Journey
Previous issue: The original homepage did not immediately communicate a distinctive reason to choose It's Beauty. The booking journey also needed greater visibility.

Implemented improvements:
- Introduced a stronger, more distinctive homepage headline.
- Highlighted the salon's main beauty services.
- Improved the visibility of the first-visit promotional offer.
- Made appointment booking a more prominent call to action.
Result: Visitors can understand the salon's positioning more quickly and find a clearer route towards booking an appointment.
2. More Consistent Trust and Credibility Information
Previous issue: The original website presented inconsistent location counts and customer review information, potentially creating confusion.

Implemented improvements:
- Presented It's Beauty as a six-location salon network across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
- Incorporated the advertised 4.9-star Google rating without relying on inconsistent review totals.
- Highlighted professional product brands and salon hygiene practices.
- Improved the presentation of business credibility information.
Result: The homepage communicates the salon's professional positioning more consistently. Exact review totals should be verified before being used in promotional content.
3. Improved Service and Pricing Navigation
Previous issue: The salon offers numerous treatments across different beauty categories, making service discovery and pricing navigation an important area for improvement.

Implemented improvements:
- Organised services into clearly identifiable categories.
- Introduced custom SVG icons for service presentation.
- Highlighted selected treatments and pricing.
- Improved access to the complete service menu and appointment booking.
Result: Visitors can more easily identify relevant treatments, understand the available services and move towards booking.
3. Design Improvements
Visual Identity
- Introduced a refined editorial-inspired visual direction.
- Applied a coordinated palette of black, lavender, sage, rose and beige.
- Combined Cormorant Garamond and Manrope typography for a balanced, premium appearance.
Layout and User Experience
- Improved homepage section hierarchy and content organisation.
- Created clearer separation between the hero, services, credibility, promotions, locations and booking sections.
- Improved visual consistency throughout the homepage.
- Made important information and booking actions easier to discover.
Service Presentation
- Added six custom SVG service icons arranged in two rows.
- Improved the visual organisation of the salon's main service categories.
- Created a more structured presentation of treatments and pricing.
Responsive Design
- Implemented responsive navigation.
- Added mobile-specific booking actions.
- Included exactly two sticky mobile call-to-action buttons.
- Adapted the layout for different screen sizes.
Image and Technical Improvements
- Defined image dimensions to improve layout stability.
- Applied object-fit: cover for consistent image presentation.
- Added lazy loading for relevant images.
- Implemented image error fallbacks and mobile image handling.
- Included SEO metadata and structured business data.
- Improved contact and booking pathways.
4. Implementation Notes
- Confirm all six branch details and individual operating schedules before publishing.
- Verify the current Google rating and review count before using specific figures in marketing materials.
- Test all images and external assets in the production hosting environment.
- Verify that booking links, contact information and mobile navigation work correctly before launch.
